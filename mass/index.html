✦ Our Journey Every Sunday ✦

The Flow of the Mass

Four parts, one big prayer — start to finish

StandSitKneel🎨 Follow the golden path down!
1
⛪
Introductory Rites

We Gather Together

🎵
Entrance & Song

The priest walks in and everyone sings to begin.

✝️
Sign of the Cross & Greeting

"In the name of the Father, and of the Son…"

🙏
We Say Sorry

"Lord, have mercy" — we ask God to forgive us.

🌟
The Gloria

A happy song of praise: "Glory to God!" (Sundays & feast days.)

📿
Opening Prayer

The priest prays for all of us together.

2
📖
Liturgy of the Word

We Listen to God

📜
First Reading

Usually a story from the Old Testament.

🎶
Responsorial Psalm

We sing or say a line back together.

✉️
Second Reading

A letter from the New Testament (Sundays).

🎉
Alleluia!

We stand and sing to welcome the Gospel.

✝️
The Gospel

A story about Jesus — the most important reading.

🗣️
Homily

The priest explains what the readings mean for us.

💛
The Creed

We say what we believe: "I believe in one God…"

🕯️
Prayers of the Faithful

We pray for the Church, the world, and people in need.

3
🍞
Liturgy of the Eucharist · The Heart of the Mass

We Give Thanks

🎁
Preparing the Gifts

Bread and wine are brought to the altar. A collection is taken.

🙌
Holy, Holy, Holy

We sing praise as the great prayer begins.

✨
The Consecration

By God's power, the bread and wine become the Body and Blood of Jesus. We kneel.

🙏
The Our Father

We pray the prayer Jesus taught us.

Open the full prayer →
🤝
Sign of Peace

"Peace be with you" — we greet those near us.

🐑
Lamb of God

We sing as we get ready to receive Jesus.

🍞
Communion

People receive Jesus in the Eucharist, then we pray quietly.

💗 This is the most special part — Jesus comes to us!
4
🕊️
Concluding Rites

We Go Out to Love

🙌
Final Blessing

The priest blesses us with the Sign of the Cross.

🚪
The Dismissal

"Go in peace." We answer: "Thanks be to God!"

Every Mass follows this same path — once you know the four parts, you'll always know where you are!
Gather → Listen → Give Thanks → Go Out
