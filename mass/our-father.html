The Our Father

We pray these words together during every Mass.

🙏

Pray Slowly and From Your Heart

Matthew 6:9–13 · Luke 11:2–4

Our Father, who art in heaven,
hallowed be thy name;
thy kingdom come;
thy will be done
on earth as it is in heaven.

Give us this day our daily bread,
and forgive us our trespasses,
as we forgive those who trespass against us;
and lead us not into temptation,
but deliver us from evil.

Amen.

What Are We Asking God?

1
God, You are our loving Father.We honor His holy name and remember that we belong to His family.
2
Let Your kingdom and Your will guide us.We ask for God's way—not just our own way—to be done.
3
Give us what we truly need today.“Daily bread” means food and every good thing we need, especially Jesus.
4
Forgive us and help us forgive.We ask for mercy while promising to show mercy to others.
5
Protect us from sin and evil.We ask God to keep us close when choices are difficult.
💛 Jesus gave us this prayer so we would always know how to speak to our Father in heaven.
Say it slowly, listen to every line, and let the words become your own prayer.
